Play The Genius Star online

The Genius Star is a tiling puzzle on a six-pointed star: seven dice decide which triangles are blocked, and eleven wooden shapes have to cover everything that is left. This page is a free browser version of it. The board is numbered 1 to 48 exactly like the printed one, the dice carry the original face numbers, and the eleven pieces are the real shapes — including the two light-blue halves that join into the Golden Star.

Nothing to install and nothing to pay for. Play a quick puzzle on your phone, take a hint when you are stuck, reveal a full solution when you give up, or open a lobby and race four friends on identical rolls.

The rules in four steps

  1. Roll the seven dice. A white star blocker lands on each of the seven numbered triangles they show.
  2. Fill every other triangle with the eleven coloured pieces. A piece snaps into place when the triangles under it are free.
  3. Turn pieces with Space or R, hold Shift to turn the other way, press F to flip. On a phone, tap a selected piece.
  4. Beat the clock. The timer stops when the last piece is down — and if the two light-blue halves form the hexagon, that is a Golden Star, a double win.

The seven dice

Four six-sided dice and three eight-sided ones, with the numbers of the original game. Together they produce 165,888 different puzzles, and cells 3, 6, 14, 35, 43 and 46 are never blocked.

The Golden Star

The two light-blue pieces each carry half of a golden star. Put them together as a hexagon inside your solution and you finish with the Golden Star whole, which counts as a double win. It is a gamble: a solver run over every possible roll shows that 97,422 of the 165,888 puzzles — 58.7 per cent — have a solution with the hexagon in one piece. The Golden? button checks the roll in front of you if you would rather not guess.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads, no trackers, no paid hints and no sign-up wall. One person builds it in his spare time, and a coffee is the whole business model.

How many puzzles are there?

165,888 — every combination the seven dice can roll. A solver in this repository has checked all of them: each one can be finished with the eleven pieces.

How often is the Golden Star possible?

In 97,422 of the 165,888 rolls, which is 58.7 per cent. The Golden Star button tells you whether the roll in front of you is one of them.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Create a lobby, share the five-letter code, and up to five players get the same roll at the same moment. The fastest finisher wins the round and the Golden Star counts double.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The board takes the full width, you drag pieces with a finger and tap a selected piece to rotate it. Nothing to install.

Do I need an account?

No. Guests can play everything, and their times stay in the browser. An account keeps your records and game history across devices.
